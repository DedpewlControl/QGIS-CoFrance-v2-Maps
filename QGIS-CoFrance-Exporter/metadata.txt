    QGIS symbology and labeling are converted to CoFrance style properties.
    Optional runway activation fields are validated and converted automatically.
    Exported coordinates are limited to six decimal places.
    Supported symbols: diamond, circle_cross, diamond_cross, triangle_hollow,
    triangle_filled, triangle_hollow_thick_bottom_border,
    triangle_with_circle_rings, circle, square, asterix, cross,
    circle_with_outer_rings, vor_classic, and ndb_classic.
    Additional navaid symbols: vor_dme, dme, vor, ndb, navaid, tacan,
    and vortac.
