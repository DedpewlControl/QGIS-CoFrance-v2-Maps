about=Exports polygon, line, symbol, and text template layers to CoFrance v2.
    QGIS symbology and labeling are converted to CoFrance style properties.
    Runway and sector activation fields are validated and converted automatically.
    Exported coordinates are limited to six decimal places.
    Supports all documented CoFrance shapes, navaids, and aerodrome symbols,
    plus per-feature z-index and zoom ranges. Matching QGIS SVG previews are
    included for dedicated radio-navigation symbols except generic navaid.
